BCA

Bachelor of Computer Applications course is a three-year undergraduate program that focuses on computer science and its applications. It covers topics such as programming, database management, networking, and software development, providing students with a solid foundation in IT. The course is ideal for those aspiring to build a career in software development, IT consulting, or systems management.
